Support

Before and after you buy.

Do the plugins work in FL Studio?

Yes. NOCTIS comes as VST3 and CLAP, so it works in FL Studio and in any DAW that supports them (Ableton, Reaper, Studio One, Cubase, Bitwig…).

Do they work on Mac?

Yes. The Mac installer includes VST3, AU (Logic and GarageBand), CLAP and a MIDI FX for Logic. It isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time right-click the installer and choose Open.

How do I activate my license?

After buying you get an email with your key. Open the plugin, press PASTE and ACTIVATE. After that it works offline: it checks your license about once a week when you're online, and keeps working up to 60 days without internet.

On how many computers can I use it?

Each license works on 1 computer. If you switch computers, press “Release this PC” on the old one and activate it on the new one. If your computer broke, write to us.

Can I try it first?

The plugins need a license to work. Watch them in action on our YouTube channel before buying.

Can I use what I make in songs I sell?

Yes, 100% royalty-free. What you create with FlowEazy plugins is yours.

Need help?

Lost key, change of computer or any question — write to us.
